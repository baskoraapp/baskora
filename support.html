Baskora Support

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Baskora is a free basketball management game for iPhone. You take charge of a club season after season: pick the five, set the tactics, make substitutions and call timeouts during matches, and build the squad in the summer market.

Getting started

  1. Choose a club. Every career starts from the same league, so a new career is a fresh run at a familiar competition.
  2. Play your next match from the Match tab. Watch it live, speed it up with the dial, or tap Simulate to go straight to the result.
  3. Between matches, use the Team tab for your squad, tactics, training and contracts, and the League tab for the table, schedule, leaders and playoffs.

A short guide is also in the app: Settings → How to Play. Settings opens from the gear on the Match tab, and from the gear on the first screen before you choose a club.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start a new career?

Open Settings with the gear on the Match tab, then tap Reset Game. This permanently deletes the current career.

Where is my career saved?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. It is saved automatically after every match and every change you make; a match in progress is saved when it ends. Deleting the app deletes the career.

How do I see more or fewer 2D clips during a match?

In Settings → Match → 2D clips, or with the camera button during a match. Both change the same setting.

Does Baskora need an internet connection?

No. The whole game runs on your iPhone. Only the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Support pages in Settings need a connection.

The app says my career could not be opened.

The saved career could not be read. Tap Start a New Career to keep playing. A copy of the old save is kept on your iPhone; if you contact us, mention it so we can help with a future update.

Can I play on iPad?

Baskora is designed for iPhone. It runs on iPad in iPhone mode.
