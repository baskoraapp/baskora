Baskora Support

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Baskora is a free basketball management game for iPhone. You take charge of a club season after season: pick the five, set the tactics, make substitutions during matches while your bench calls the timeouts, and build the squad in the summer signings.

Getting started

  1. Take the tour, or skip it. The first time the app opens, ten pages walk through the tabs, with a game playing on the 2D court. Swipe or tap Next; Skip at the top right closes it.
  2. Choose a club. Every career starts from the same league, so a new career is a fresh run at a familiar competition.
  3. Play your next match from the Match tab. Watch it live and speed it up with the dial, or tap Simulate to go straight to the result. During a live match, press and hold Simulate on the dial until it fills to finish the game at once.
  4. Between matches, use the Team tab for your squad, tactics, training and contracts, and the League tab for the table, schedule, leaders and playoffs.

A short guide is also in the app: Settings → How to Play. Settings opens from the gear on the Match tab, and from the gear on the first screen before you choose a club.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start a new career?

Open Settings with the gear on the Match tab, then tap Reset Game. This permanently deletes the current career.

How do I see the opening tour again?

Open Settings and tap Show the tour, under About. The ten pages from the first launch play again, with a game on the 2D court; Skip closes them.

Where is my career saved?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. It is saved automatically after every match and every change you make. A finished match is saved when you tap Continue, or as soon as you leave the app; a match left halfway is not saved and is played again. Deleting the app deletes the career.

How do I share my season or my career?

In the summer, Share the season is at the very bottom of the Match tab; a finished season opened from Stats → Career has its own. Share the career is at the bottom of Stats → Career. The picture is made on your iPhone, in the shape of a photo, and goes only where you send it. Save Image asks your permission to add it to Photos.

How do I see more or fewer 2D clips during a match?

In Settings → Match → 2D clips, or with the camera button during a match. Both change the same setting.

Does Baskora need an internet connection?

No. The whole game runs on your iPhone. Only the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Support pages in Settings need a connection, and the tip card at the top of Settings, which shows only when the App Store can be reached.

What are the tips in Settings?

Assist the Team, at the top of Settings, lets you leave an optional tip — Free Throw, Three Pointer or Slam Dunk — to support the game. Each tip is a single payment, not a subscription, and you can leave one as often as you like. A tip unlocks nothing and changes nothing in the game. Payment is handled by Apple; for a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

The app says my career could not be opened.

The saved career could not be read. Tap Start a New Career to keep playing. A copy of the old save is kept on your iPhone; if you contact us, mention it so we can help with a future update.

Can I play on iPad?

Baskora is designed for iPhone. It runs on iPad in iPhone mode.
