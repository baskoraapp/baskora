Baskora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Baskora is a basketball management game for iPhone, developed by Ceyhun Asılkazancı. The game runs entirely on your device and needs no internet connection. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app and these web pages.

Information we collect

None. Baskora does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no account to create, and the app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party software that could do any of these.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, Baskora keeps the following on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage:

  • Your career: the club you manage, its players, results and season history, and the 2D clips setting you play with.
  • Your match preferences: the default match speed, what happens at a quarter break, and the 2D clips setting you choose before starting a career.

This information never leaves your device through Baskora, and the developer has no access to it. It may be included in backups you make of your iPhone with iCloud or a computer; those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your career is kept on your iPhone until you remove it. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset Game inside the app. Deleting the app removes your career and your preferences.

Cookies

Baskora uses no cookies. These web pages set no cookies, run no scripts and load nothing from other websites.

These web pages

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Support links in the app's Settings open these pages inside the app, in Safari's in-app view. Opening them is the only time Baskora uses the internet, and the app sends no information of its own when it does.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. The developer does not receive these logs. For more information, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Baskora does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date at the top.
